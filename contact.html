FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is this an official Marvel Studios website? ▼
No. This website is strictly an independent, non-commercial fan creative project developed for educational, portfolio demonstration, and entertainment purposes. All characters and trademarks remain the property of Marvel Characters, Inc. and The Walt Disney Company.
Should I watch the MCU in Release Order or Chronological Order? ▼
If you are experiencing the MCU for the very first time, we strongly recommend Theatrical Release Order so you experience end-credits revelations and character evolutions naturally. For repeat marathons, Chronological Order offers a magnificent historical perspective starting in WWII with Captain America.
How often is new movie data and trailers updated? ▼
Our structured database files (data/characters.js and data/movies.js) are continuously updated whenever Marvel Studios releases official trailers, box office receipts, or casting confirmations.
