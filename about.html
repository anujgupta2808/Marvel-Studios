THE SAGA UNVEILED

BEHIND THE UNIVERSE

An unprecedented achievement in cinematic worldbuilding. How an independent studio gambled on a gold-and-crimson armored hero and built the greatest interconnected storytelling franchise in modern history.

2008 — 2019

THE INFINITY SAGA

It began with a bold gamble: Marvel Studios mortgaged their remaining comic characters to produce Iron Man (2008), directed by Jon Favreau and starring Robert Downey Jr. That film's post-credits scene, featuring Samuel L. Jackson's Nick Fury declaring "You've become part of a bigger universe," forever changed cinema.

Across 23 interconnected films spanning Phases 1 through 3, the Avengers assembled, defended Earth against alien invasions, fractured in Civil War, and endured unimaginable tragedy in Infinity War, culminating in the record-breaking global sensation Avengers: Endgame.

Pillars of the Infinity Saga

  • Phase 1: Avengers Assembled

    Origins of Iron Man, Hulk, Thor, and Captain America leading to the historic Battle of New York (2012).

  • Phase 2: Cosmic & Subatomic Expansion

    Guardians of the Galaxy and Ant-Man expand the sandbox to outer space and the Quantum Realm.

  • Phase 3: The Climax of Destiny

    Civil War, Black Panther, Infinity War, and Endgame — generating over $13.5 Billion worldwide.

2021 — PRESENT

THE MULTIVERSE SAGA

Following the temporal disruption of Endgame and Loki's ascension at the Citadel at the End of Time, the Sacred Timeline branched into infinite realities.

Phases 4, 5, and 6 explore the dangers of multiversal incursions, bringing together legacy characters in Spider-Man: No Way Home and Deadpool & Wolverine, and preparing humanity for upcoming clashes in Avengers: Doomsday and Avengers: Secret Wars.

Multiversal Frontiers

  • Branching Dimensions & Incursions

    Alternate variants, TVA governance, and cosmic incursions colliding parallel Earths.

  • Arrival of Mutants & First Family

    The historic integration of Wolverine, Deadpool, and The Fantastic Four into the mainline MCU.

  • Road to Secret Wars

    The Russo Brothers return to helm Avengers: Doomsday and Secret Wars.
